    About this fictional case and its sources

    Noah is 22 and has Duchenne muscular dystrophy. In the supplied earlier case, he used nocturnal non-invasive ventilation and assisted cough. This game imagines a later point with an established tracheostomy: its reason, timing, tube details and upper-airway patency are not established. The case is separate from the supplied Maya and Daniel cases.

    Designed from the supplied Project Hope tabletop game, action-station references and tracheostomy material. Sources include NTSP resources and the NSW ACI adult tracheostomy guide. The supplied paediatric teaching package is not an adult protocol.

    Safety and access

    Educational prototype requiring local clinical and disability-led review. It is not a clinical decision tool or proof of competence. It does not give procedural steps, medication doses or equipment settings. In an actual emergency use the individual plan, trained responders and local pathway.

    Use keyboard or switch scanning; all choices are untimed. Communication access, privacy, patient decision-making and a way to say WAIT remain visible. Silence does not establish consent, refusal, incapacity or symptom absence. No game control connects to clinical equipment.
