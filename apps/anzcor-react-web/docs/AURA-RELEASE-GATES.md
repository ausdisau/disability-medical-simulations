# AURA release gates

1. Verify current ANZCOR paediatric guidance and obtain age-appropriate **NSW local paediatric** procedure, medication and advanced airway protocol pack.
2. Qualified paediatric ALS, PICU and anaesthesia review of all intervention stations, outcomes and clinician-authorization semantics.
3. Paid disability-led personhood, AAC, baseline-vs-deterioration and family role review; protect `WAIT` and prohibit no-response-to-incapacity.
4. Clinical treatment, medication and defibrillation gates must never be unlocked merely by equipment availability, UI controls or facilitator preference.
5. Confirm deterministic reproducibility, historical event provenance, event immutability and no model-origin physiology.
6. Test keyboard, switch scanning, screen-reader, low-sensory, reduced-motion, text scaling and 200–400% zoom. Expose no inaccessible countdown timers.
7. Verify static-host isolation; forbid real medical streams, identifiers and telemetry. No browser storage or remote persistence in the preview.
8. Require separate human sign-off before merge, deployment or training use; no NSW Health endorsement claim.

Canonical codebase remains unchanged outside `apps/anzcor-react-web/`.
