# Review status · Noah ICU v0.3

Engineering prototype; no clinical validation or competence certification is claimed.

The release preserves separate individual baseline, clinical change, communication access and system readiness. CP and DMD are distinct fictional profiles; DMD is not generalized to every muscular dystrophy. Adult cases do not inherit paediatric algorithms or a paediatric narrative outcome.

Clinical review should concentrate on the simplified gas/circulation assumptions, pressure and volume labels, manual-bag mechanics, arrest-specific validity limits, waveform interpretation, emergency versus planned-care gates, and evidence-version applicability. The model intentionally lacks full ALS, rhythm diagnosis, procedural airway insertion/replacement, pharmacology, prognosis and automatic ROSC.

Disability-led review should examine patient voice, unknown responses, WAIT, scoped agreement, communication access, autonomy, respectful positioning and chosen supporters. Usability review should include screen readers, switch access, keyboard, zoom, contrast, reduced motion and small screens. Implemented features are not a claim of completed WCAG or assistive-technology certification.

The evidence mapping and numerical MODEL.md state what is sourced, what is an engineering assumption and what remains outside validity. Formal teaching deployment requires review of those concrete artifacts rather than treating passing code tests as medical validation.
